# Music

`soft-pad.mp3` is synthesised by `tools/music.py` (no licence needed).

The other tracks are by **Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They were loudness-normalised (-14 LUFS) for mixing; the music itself is unchanged.

**Every video that uses one of them must credit it in its description**, e.g.:

```
Music: "Dreamer" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
```
