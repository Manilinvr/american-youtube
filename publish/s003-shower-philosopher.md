## Описание

```
Ever solve a problem in the shower that beat you all week? Congratulations. You're Specimen #003: The Shower Philosopher (Homo balneosophus).

Scientists call it the shower effect. In a 2022 study, more than 300 people took a creativity test, then watched a video: either two men hanging laundry, or a scene from a movie. Only with the mildly interesting video did a wandering mind lead to more creative ideas. So the secret isn't boredom. It's a task that's just interesting enough to let your mind wander.

Try it: a walk, washing dishes, a shower. Not your phone. That one's too interesting.

Maybe the answer doesn't need more effort. Just a little space. Perfectly human.

Which specimen are you? Tell us in the comments.

Sources:
- Irving, Z. C., McGrath, C., Flynn, L., Glasser, A., & Mills, C. (2022). The shower effect: Mind wandering facilitates creative incubation during moderately engaging activities. Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts.
- Baird, B., Smallwood, J., Mrazek, M. D., Kam, J. W. Y., Franklin, M. S., & Schooler, J. W. (2012). Inspired by distraction: Mind wandering facilitates creative incubation. Psychological Science, 23(10), 1117-1122.
```
