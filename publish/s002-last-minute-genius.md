## Описание

```
Ever do more the day before vacation than all month? Congratulations. You're Specimen #002: The Last-Minute Genius (Homo procrastinans).

Meet Parkinson's law: work expands to fill the time you give it. Historian C. Northcote Parkinson wrote it in 1955 as a joke in The Economist, poking fun at government offices that kept growing while the British Empire shrank. Then scientists tested it. In a 1967 experiment, people given extra time on a task really did take longer, as if more time quietly lowered their goals.

Use it against yourself: give the task less time. Set a timer. Bring the deadline closer.

You're not lazy. Your deadline was just too far away. Perfectly human.

Which specimen are you? Tell us in the comments.

Sources:

- Bryan, J. F., & Locke, E. A. (1967). Parkinson's law as a goal-setting phenomenon. Organizational Behavior and Human Performance, 2(3), 258-275.
```
