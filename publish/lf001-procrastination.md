2. ОПИСАНИЕ (вставить целиком в поле «Описание»)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
You know the task matters. You care about it. So why is it the one thing you can't make yourself start?

This video looks at what psychology research says is really going on when you put off the things you care about most. The short answer: procrastination is often less about time or laziness and more about avoiding a feeling. You'll see the experiments behind that idea, why caring more can make a task feel scarier, why "future you" gets stuck with the bill, and what the evidence says actually helps (and how strong that evidence is).
