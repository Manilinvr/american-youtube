ПРАВИЛА НАПОЛНЕНИЯ КАДРА — вставлять в КАЖДЫЙ промпт сцены
====================================================================
Порядок промпта:
[STYLE] + [CHARACTER] + [SCENE DRESSING — блок ниже, целиком] + LOCATION + ACTION + PROPS + LIGHT + CAMERA + [Negative]


[SCENE DRESSING — копировать дословно]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE DRESSING: the room is a real, lived-in place with believable detail, not an empty set. Build the frame in three layers:
FOREGROUND — one or two real objects close to the camera, softly out of focus, partly framing the shot (the edge of a mug, a plant leaf, a chair back, a stack of books).
MIDGROUND — the professor and the one main prop he is physically interacting with, sharp and clearly readable; his hands visibly touch, hold, push, open or look at it.
BACKGROUND — 4 to 7 pieces of real decor that match the location and the topic (shelves with books and small objects, framed pictures without readable text, a lamp, curtains, plants, a coat on a hook, a window with a view), slightly blurred.
Every object must have a reason to be there: it either belongs naturally to this location or supports the idea of the scene. Small story details are welcome (a half-drunk coffee, an unopened envelope, a phone face down, crumpled notes). Keep the frame organised: clear path for the eye to the professor's face and the main prop, no random clutter, no floating objects, no duplicated items, no readable text on any surface.


[LOCATION — менять каждые 2–3 кадра, не повторять одну локацию больше 4 кадров подряд]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Дом:      cosy living room with bookshelves · small kitchen with a window · bedroom at night · home desk corner by a window · hallway with coats and a mirror · bathroom mirror in the morning · balcony with plants at dusk
Работа:   open-plan office · small meeting room with a whiteboard · coworking café · university lecture hall · professor's study with old books · library reading room
Город:    coffee shop by the window · park bench in autumn · subway car · bus stop in the rain · bookshop aisle · rooftop at sunset · laundromat at night
Наука:    small psychology lab with a table and two chairs · lecture stage with a chalkboard (no readable writing) · archive room with files


[PROPS — один главный предмет на кадр, с которым он взаимодействует; выбирать по смыслу фразы]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Откладывание / задачи:   laptop with an unopened email, sticky notes, calendar, to-do notebook, alarm clock, pile of paperwork
Чувства / тревога:       cup of tea, blanket, phone face down, rain on the window, crumpled letter, journal and pen
Исследования:            clipboard, stopwatch, folders, glass jars, small puzzle, handheld game, magazines, chalk
Отношения / люди:        two cups on a table, phone with a message, photo frame (no faces), umbrella shared
Отдых / решения:         armchair and book, record player, plant he is watering, window he opens, coffee he pours
Объяснение идей:         chalkboard with simple drawings, pointer stick, desk globe, magnifying glass, model of a staircase


[ACTION — глагол обязателен]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Каждый кадр: он ДЕЛАЕТ что-то с главным предметом — holds, opens, pushes away, reaches for, writes in, pours, points at, turns off, picks up, looks closely through.
Не «stands next to a laptop», а «pushes the laptop lid halfway closed with one finger, eyebrows raised».


ПРИМЕР ГОТОВОГО ПРОМПТА СЦЕНЫ
====================================================================
[STYLE] + [CHARACTER] + [SCENE DRESSING]
LOCATION: a small real kitchen in the morning.
ACTION: the professor pushes an unopened white envelope away across the table with one finger while reaching for his coffee with the other hand, eyebrows slightly guilty.
PROPS: main prop — the envelope; foreground — a blurred glass jar of cookies on the left edge; background — a window with morning light, a kettle, hanging mugs, a small potted herb, a wall calendar with crossed-out days (no numbers), a jacket on the chair.
LIGHT: warm sunlight from the window behind him, soft shadows.
CAMERA: medium shot at table height, shallow depth of field.
[Negative]


ДОБАВИТЬ В NEGATIVE
====================================================================
empty room, plain background, studio backdrop, random clutter, floating objects, duplicated objects, readable text, letters on signs, logos, brand names
