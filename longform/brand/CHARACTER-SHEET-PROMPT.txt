ОПИСАНИЕ ПЕРСОНАЖА (одинаковое во всех промптах — уже вставлено ниже)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A friendly cartoon psychology professor drawn in a clean flat 2D animation style: bold smooth black outlines of even weight, flat colours with simple two-tone cel shading (one soft shadow tone), no textures, no gradients, no 3D.
Head: large, slightly wide rounded head with a soft broad jaw, about one third of his total height; warm peach skin (#F2D3B3) with a slightly darker peach shadow on one side; small rounded ears.
Hair: short jet-black hair, swept up and back, with a few small spiky tufts sticking out at the back of the crown; drawn as one solid black shape.
Glasses: large perfectly round glasses with thin black frames, always on.
Eyes: white eyes with small dark pupils and heavy half-closed upper eyelids, giving a relaxed, knowing, slightly amused look; thin arched black eyebrows above the frames.
Nose: a single curved black line shaped like a hook, starting between the eyes.
Mouth: a small lopsided friendly smile (a small dark open mouth when talking).
Hands: cartoon hands with four fingers, same peach skin.
Clothes (flat colours, same outline style): black turtleneck sweater, charcoal-grey blazer with notched lapels and two flap pockets, slim black trousers, black sneakers with white soles and three white stripes on the side.
Body: slim, average height, relaxed posture.
