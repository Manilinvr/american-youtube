# lf001 · Визуальный стиль и герой (этап 4, часть 1)

## Карточка стиля

**STYLE:** original hand-drawn 2D editorial cartoon illustration, 16:9 composition, expressive black ink outlines with subtle natural imperfections, flat muted colors, restrained paper grain, simple readable shapes, thoughtful visual storytelling, clean composition, no 3D, no photorealism, no glossy AI aesthetic, no gradients, no text.

**HERO (повторять дословно в каждом промпте сцены):** a recurring minimalist adult human character with a large round plain warm-white head, simple expressive eyes with small black pupils, carefully drawn eyebrows, a small line mouth, no nose, no hair, thin black line arms and legs, a simple recognizable silhouette, wearing an oversized dusty-teal crewneck sweater, charcoal trousers and simple off-white sneakers. Emotions are shown through eyebrows, eyes, mouth, posture and hands, never through exaggerated facial distortion.

**SUPPORTING CHARACTERS:** people in the same hand-drawn style — same round warm-white heads, same eyes and line limbs — but with varied simple hairstyles, clothing colors from the palette, ages and body language, so they read as part of the same illustrated world and never look like the hero.

**VISUAL METAPHORS (сквозные для этого ролика):**
- *the email* — a plain white envelope with a small ochre seal; it grows heavier and larger as days pass;
- *dread* — a small charcoal scribble cloud hovering near the hero's head; it shrinks at the end;
- *the backpack stone* — a grey stone in the hero's backpack that gets bigger each day;
- *the loop* — a hand-drawn circular arrow path with five stops;
- *the inner critic* — a tall charcoal shadow-figure holding a megaphone behind the hero's shoulder;
- *future self* — the same hero drawn as a pale, slightly faded outline standing a few steps away.
Метафоры не выдаются за буквальный механизм мозга; никаких изображений мозга и нейронов в этом ролике.

**PALETTE:** warm off-white (#F4EEE3) background, beige (#E3D5BF), muted blue (#7C93A6), dusty teal (#5F8F8B), ochre (#C9963B), charcoal (#2E2E2E), soft terracotta (#C46F57), restrained red accent (#B4382F) used only for one key detail per image. Scenes of avoidance and guilt lean cooler (muted blue, charcoal); moments of insight and the final scene lean warmer (ochre, terracotta).

**COMPOSITION:** one dominant idea per image, clear silhouettes, readable expressions, simple backgrounds, generous negative space, strong visual hierarchy, original editorial illustration rather than stock imagery.

**CONSISTENCY:** same head shape and size ratio (head about one third of body height), same eye design, same line weight, same sweater color and proportions in every frame.

**Надписи:** текст (цифры, карточки, цитаты) добавляется в монтаже. В генерации — никаких букв.
